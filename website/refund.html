1. Who bills you

Vitrack Pro subscriptions are sold and billed by Paddle.com Market Ltd ("Paddle"), acting as our Merchant of Record and authorized reseller. Your receipt, invoice, and any charge on your statement come from Paddle, not directly from Vitrack's operator. Paddle's own Buyer Terms and Checkout Buyer Terms of Sale (available at paddle.com/legal) apply to the purchase transaction itself, alongside this policy.

2. Free tier

Vitrack's core features (unlimited photo analysis, calendar tracking, and weekly insights) are free and require no payment, so there is nothing to refund on the free tier.

3. Subscription renewals

A Pro subscription renews automatically at the end of each billing period (monthly or yearly, depending on the plan you chose) at the then-current price, unless you cancel before the renewal date. Cancelling stops future renewals but does not, by itself, entitle you to a refund of a period you've already paid for.

Where Israeli Consumer Protection Law, 1981 and its regulations (including any right to cancel a distance sale transaction), or another mandatory consumer-protection law applicable to you, grants a non-waivable cancellation or refund right, that right is honored in full, notwithstanding the above.

Contact us at shilohdhd1@gmail.com with your Paddle order/transaction ID (found in your receipt email) and the reason for the request. You can also contact Paddle directly through their own support channels, since they hold your payment and order records as Merchant of Record.

Approved refunds are processed by Paddle back to your original payment method, in line with the timeframe set out in Paddle's own Buyer Terms (paddle.com/legal).
